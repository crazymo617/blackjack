Table rules
Strategy follows the rules: the book, Ask the dealer, the chart, the Book comparison and the simulator all use the correct basic strategy for these rules.

Shuffle: the full deck order is set once per shuffle and dealt from the top in exactly that order. You and the Book always get the same new deck at each shuffle. On a reshuffle, the discards and undealt cards are gathered and hand-shuffled: riffle, riffle, strip, riffle, cut, burn one. The first shuffle starts with a wash (new decks scrambled on the felt).

Always: insurance pays 2 to 1, and the dealer checks for blackjack under an ace or ten before you play.
